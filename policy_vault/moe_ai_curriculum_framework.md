# MoE AI Curriculum Framework

**Document Reference:** MSOE-AI-CURR-2026-03  
**Effective Date:** 01 January 2026  
**Review Cycle:** Biennial  
**Owner:** Director of Curriculum Innovation (Ministry of Education — UAE)  
**Jurisdiction:** UAE Federal curriculum track; adoptable by British, IB, and
CBSE tracks as an enrichment strand

---

## 1. Vision Statement

The UAE Ministry of Education AI Curriculum Framework prepares every learner
to **understand, apply, and critically evaluate artificial intelligence** as a
foundational literacy of the 21st century. The framework develops computational
thinking, ethical reasoning, and human-centred design capabilities from Grade 1
through Grade 12, in alignment with UAE Centennial 2071 and the National
Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2031.

## 2. Guiding Principles

1. **Human-centred AI.** Technology serves people; learners study AI's social
   impact alongside its technical mechanics.
2. **Ethics by design.** Fairness, transparency, privacy, and accountability
   are taught as engineering requirements, not afterthoughts.
3. **Inclusive participation.** Every learner, regardless of gender, ability,
   or socioeconomic background, has equitable access to AI education.
4. **Bilingual capability.** Learners develop AI literacy in both Arabic and
   English, including technical vocabulary in both languages.
5. **Evidence-based practice.** Pedagogical approaches are validated through
   classroom research and national assessment data.

## 3. Progression Model

| Stage | Grades | Focus | Weekly Allocation |
|-------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Foundation | 1–3 | Pattern recognition, sequencing, smart systems in daily life | 45 minutes |
| Explorer | 4–6 | Block-based coding, data collection, simple classification | 60 minutes |
| Builder | 7–9 | Python fundamentals, supervised learning concepts, datasets | 90 minutes |
| Innovator | 10–12 | Model training, NLP / computer vision projects, AI ethics capstone | 120 minutes |

## 4. Strand Architecture

### Strand A — Computational Thinking
Decomposition, abstraction, algorithmic design, debugging, and complexity
awareness. Delivered through unplugged activities in early years and
progressive coding environments thereafter.

### Strand B — Data Literacy
Data generation, cleaning, visualisation, bias detection, and governance.
Learners work with authentic open datasets while applying UAE Personal Data
Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) principles.

### Strand C — Machine Learning Concepts
Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning introduced age-
appropriately. Grades 10–12 implement end-to-end pipelines (train / validate /
deploy) using accessible frameworks.

### Strand D — Natural Language Processing
Text and speech processing with emphasis on Arabic language technologies,
including morphology-aware tokenisation and dialectal variation challenges.

### Strand E — AI Ethics and Society
Algorithmic bias, explainability, labour-market impact, intellectual property,
autonomous systems governance, and UAE AI Ethics Guidelines.

### Strand F — Human-Centred Design
Problem discovery, stakeholder research, prototyping, and usability testing
for AI-assisted products and services.

## 5. Assessment Framework

| Mode | Weight (Grades 10–12) | Description |
|------|-----------------------|-------------|
| Portfolio | 30% | Cumulative artefacts documenting learning growth |
| Practical project | 35% | End-to-end AI solution addressing a real community need |
| Ethics case study | 20% | Structured analysis of an AI deployment scenario |
| Written examination | 15% | Conceptual understanding and critical evaluation |

Formative assessment tools include rubric-scored coding notebooks, peer code
review, and reflective journals.

## 6. Educator Development

- **Level 1 (All teachers):** AI Awareness micro-credential (12 hours).
- **Level 2 (AI strand teachers):** Applied AI Pedagogy certificate (40 hours).
- **Level 3 (Lead practitioners):** Curriculum Design & Mentoring diploma
  (120 hours + supervised practicum).

National professional learning communities meet quarterly; a shared resource
repository is maintained by the MoE Curriculum Directorate.

## 7. Technology and Infrastructure Standards

Schools delivering this framework must provide:

- Managed devices with sufficient compute for browser-based ML environments
- Reliable broadband (minimum 10 Mbps per classroom cluster)
- Secure sandboxed cloud workspaces for model training (student accounts only)
- Local datasets compliant with UAE data residency requirements
- Assistive technology accommodations for persons of determination

Procurement of AI tools requires a completed **AI Tool Impact Assessment
(ATIA-01)** covering data handling, age-appropriateness, and accessibility.

## 8. Safeguarding and Data Protection

- Student data used in AI projects must be anonymised or synthetic wherever
  possible.
- No biometric or facial recognition datasets of real students are permitted
  in classroom projects.
- Generative AI tools used in class must be age-gated and filtered; student
  prompts are logged for audit purposes.
- Parental consent is required for any project publishing student work
  outside the school environment.

## 9. Industry and Community Partnerships

The framework encourages partnerships with UAE technology sector organisations,
universities, and government accelerators for mentorship, guest instruction,
and challenge-based learning. All partners complete safeguarding vetting and
sign the MoE Partnership Data Charter.

## 10. Monitoring and Evaluation

| Indicator | Target (2026–2027) | Frequency |
|-----------|--------------------|-----------|
| Learners completing Strand F capstone | 85% of Grade 12 cohort | Annual |
| Teachers at Level 2 or above | 60% of AI strand staff | Annual |
| Gender parity in advanced AI electives | 50% ± 5% | Annual |
| Student ethics case study mean score | ≥ 70% | Annual |
| Schools with ATIA-01 on file for all AI tools | 100% | Termly |

Results are reported to the MoE Curriculum Directorate and published in the
National Education Outcomes Report.

## Appendix A — Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Algorithm | A finite sequence of well-defined instructions for solving a problem |
| Bias (AI) | Systematic error in model outputs that unfairly disadvantages groups |
| Inference | Using a trained model to make predictions on new data |
| Model | A mathematical representation learned from data |
| Tokenisation | Splitting text into units (words, subwords) for processing |
