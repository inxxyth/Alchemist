# KHDA / ADEK Health & Safety Protocols

**Document Reference:** MSOE-HS-2026-02  
**Effective Date:** 01 September 2025  
**Review Cycle:** Annual (or after any major incident)  
**Owner:** Head of Campus Operations & Compliance  
**Jurisdiction:** UAE — Dubai (KHDA) and Abu Dhabi (ADEK) regulated schools

---

## 1. Regulatory Basis

This protocol consolidates the health, safety, and wellbeing requirements
enforced by:

- **KHDA** — Knowledge and Human Development Authority (Dubai)
- **ADEK** — Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge
- **MoHAP** — Ministry of Health and Prevention (communicable disease control)
- **Civil Defence** — Fire and life safety codes for educational occupancies
- **Federal Law No. 8 of 1980** and amendments concerning occupational safety

Schools operating under this framework are subject to unannounced inspections
and must evidence compliance through the MSOE Health & Safety Register.

## 2. Governance Structure

Every campus maintains a Health & Safety Committee comprising:

- Principal (accountable person)
- Head of Campus Operations (responsible person)
- Lead Nurse / School Health Coordinator
- Facilities Manager
- Parent Representative
- Student Council Representative (secondary schools)

The committee meets **monthly** and publishes minutes to the school's
compliance portal within five working days.

## 3. Risk Assessment Framework

All activities — on-site and off-site — require a documented risk assessment
prior to commencement using form **HS-RA-01**.

| Severity | Likelihood | Action |
|----------|------------|--------|
| High | High | Prohibit activity until controls reduce residual risk to Medium or below |
| High | Medium | Senior leadership sign-off + enhanced supervision |
| Medium | Medium | Standard controls + documented briefing |
| Low | Low | Monitor; review annually |

Risk assessments are reviewed:

- Before the start of each academic term
- After any near-miss or incident
- When facilities, equipment, or activities change

## 4. Campus Health Services

### 4.1 Clinical Staffing

- Every campus with more than 500 students retains a full-time MoHAP-licensed
  school nurse during operating hours.
- Clinics are equipped for first response, chronic condition management
  (asthma, diabetes, epilepsy), and emergency stabilisation.
- Medication administration requires a signed Medication Consent Form (MC-02)
  and must be stored in the clinic's locked cabinet.

### 4.2 Vaccination and Health Records

- Immunisation records are mandatory for enrolment (UAE National Immunisation
  Programme schedule).
- Expired or missing vaccination documentation triggers a **30-day remediation
  window**, after which the student is placed on the Health Compliance Hold list.
- Communicable disease notifications are filed with MoHAP within 24 hours of
  diagnosis for notifiable conditions (measles, meningitis, tuberculosis,
  COVID-19 clusters, etc.).

### 4.3 Allergies and Special Dietary Needs

- An Allergen Register is maintained per campus and shared with catering.
- Anaphylaxis-ready emergency kits (adrenaline auto-injectors) are stationed
  at no fewer than one point per building.
- Staff complete annual allergy awareness training.

## 5. Fire, Evacuation, and Civil Defence

| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| Evacuation drills | Minimum 2 per term (one announced, one unannounced) |
| Assembly points | Marked, signed, and capacity-checked per campus |
| Fire wardens | Minimum 1 per floor / zone; trained annually |
| Detection systems | Full coverage; quarterly functional testing |
| Extinguishers | Serviced annually; logged in HS-FIRE-03 |
| Lift evacuation | Procedure documented for persons of determination |

Maximum permitted evacuation time for a full campus is **8 minutes** from
alarm to roll-call completion.

## 6. Facilities and Premises Standards

- **Temperature:** Indoor learning spaces maintained between 22–25°C.
- **Air quality:** HVAC filters replaced quarterly; CO₂ monitoring in
  high-occupancy rooms (threshold 1,000 ppm).
- **Water:** Drinking water outlets tested twice yearly for potability.
- **Playgrounds / sports:** Impact-absorbing surfaces inspected weekly;
  equipment load-tested annually.
- **Persons of determination:** Full accessibility compliance per Dubai
  Universal Design Code / Abu Dhabi Building Code accessibility sections.

## 7. School Transport Safety

- Buses comply with RTA / ADEK transport specifications (GPS, cameras,
  child-presence detection, speed limiters).
- Driver and attendant vetting includes annual medical fitness and
  defensive-driving certification.
- The "last child off" protocol requires a physical seat sweep with
  time-stamped checklist (TR-07) at the end of every route.

## 8. Incident Management and Reporting

1. **Immediate response:** Administer first aid; secure the scene; notify
   the Lead Nurse and Principal.
2. **Same-day logging:** File Incident Report IR-01 in the MSOE compliance
   system within 4 hours.
3. **Regulatory notification:**
   - Serious injury / hospitalisation → KHDA or ADEK within 24 hours
   - Fatality → immediate notification to regulator, police, and MoHAP
   - Foodborne illness cluster → Dubai Municipality / ADAFSA within 4 hours
4. **Post-incident review:** Root-cause analysis completed within 10 working
   days; corrective actions tracked to closure.

## 9. Emergency Preparedness

Campuses maintain and exercise the following scenario plans:

- Fire and explosion
- Medical emergency / mass casualty
- Severe weather / sandstorm
- Security lockdown (intruder protocol)
- Utility failure (power, water, network)
- Pandemic / public health emergency (shift to distance learning)

Emergency contact trees are tested quarterly and after any staffing change.
