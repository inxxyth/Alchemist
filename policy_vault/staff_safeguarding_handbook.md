# Staff Safeguarding Handbook

**Document Reference:** MSOE-SAFEGUARD-2026-01  
**Effective Date:** 01 September 2025  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Owner:** Director of Student Welfare & Child Protection  
**Jurisdiction:** United Arab Emirates — Dubai / Abu Dhabi / Northern Emirates

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This handbook establishes the mandatory safeguarding standards for all teaching,
non-teaching, contractor, and volunteer personnel operating within MSOE member
schools. It aligns with UAE Federal Law No. 3 of 2016 (Wadeema's Law), the
Ministry of Education Child Protection Policy, and KHDA/ADEK inspection
framework requirements for student wellbeing.

The handbook applies to:

- All employed staff (full-time, part-time, and temporary)
- Supply teachers and educational consultants
- Transport, catering, and facilities contractors
- Parent volunteers and chaperones on school premises or school-organised trips

## 2. Core Principles

1. **The welfare of the child is paramount.** No operational, reputational, or
   commercial consideration overrides a child's immediate safety.
2. **Zero tolerance for abuse.** Physical, emotional, sexual, and neglectful
   harm are grounds for immediate suspension and referral to authorities.
3. **Safe recruitment.** All personnel undergo enhanced background checks,
   including police clearance certificates from the UAE and any country of
   prior residence within the last five years.
4. **Culture of vigilance.** Staff are required to report concerns even when
   they are uncertain; the designated safeguarding lead determines escalation.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) | Single point of contact; maintains the secure concern log; liaises with KHDA/ADEK and police Child Protection Units |
| Deputy DSL | Acts in the DSL's absence; supports front-line staff |
| All Staff | Recognise indicators of harm; report within 24 hours; maintain confidentiality |
| Governing Body | Annual policy review; ensures DSL capacity and training budget |
| Parents / Guardians | Cooperate with safeguarding enquiries; report concerns to the DSL |

## 4. Recognition of Concern

Staff must be alert to indicators including but not limited to:

- Unexplained injuries, burns, or bruising in patterns inconsistent with play
- Deterioration in hygiene, nutrition, or attendance
- Age-inappropriate sexualised behaviour or knowledge
- Sudden withdrawal, aggression, or self-harm ideation
- Disclosure of domestic violence, substance misuse, or exploitation
- Online grooming indicators (secretive device use, unexplained gifts)

## 5. Reporting Procedure

1. **Immediate danger:** Call emergency services (999 / 998) and secure the child.
2. **Non-immediate concern:** Record factual observations on the same day using
   the school's Safeguarding Concern Form (SC-01). Do not interview the child.
3. Submit the form to the DSL within **24 hours** via the secure staff portal.
4. The DSL acknowledges receipt within one working day and determines the
   pathway: internal support plan, external referral, or dual track.
5. All referrals to the Ministry of Interior Child Protection Unit are filed
   through the designated KHDA/ADEK reporting portal within 48 hours.

## 6. Confidentiality and Information Sharing

Concern records are stored in a restricted-access register retained until the
student's 25th birthday. Information is shared on a need-to-know basis with:

- The DSL and Deputy DSL
- External child protection authorities
- Receiving schools (under formal transfer protocol)

Staff must never discuss concerns in open staff rooms, messaging groups, or
with other parents.

## 7. Safer Working Practices

- Maintain professional boundaries at all times; avoid one-to-one situations
  behind closed doors.
- Physical contact is limited to incidental care or agreed support plans.
- Personal social media contact with students is prohibited.
- Photography and video require documented parental consent (form PC-04).
- Mobile phone use in student areas is restricted to school-issued devices.

## 8. Training Requirements

| Audience | Frequency | Provider |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| All staff | Annual induction + refresher | Internal DSL / KHDA-approved provider |
| DSL and Deputy | Level 3 multi-agency training (annual) | External accredited body |
| Board members | Biennial safeguarding briefing | Internal DSL |
| Contractors | Site induction (safeguarding module) | Facilities Manager |

## 9. Allegations Against Staff

Any allegation against a member of staff is reported immediately to the
Principal and the Chair of the Governing Body. The individual is suspended
pending investigation. External investigations are coordinated with the
relevant Emirates police Child Protection Unit. A single central record
(Case Register AL-01) is maintained by the Principal's office.

## 10. Documentation Retention

| Record | Retention |
|--------|-----------|
| Safeguarding Concern Forms | Until student's 25th birthday |
| Allegation files | 10 years from case closure |
| Training certificates | 6 years after staff departure |
| Recruitment vetting records | 6 years after staff departure |
