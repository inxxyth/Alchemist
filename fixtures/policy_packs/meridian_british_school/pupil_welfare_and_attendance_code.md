## 1. Scope of this Code

This Code sets the pastoral and operational rules that apply to every pupil on
roll at the school, and to every member of staff, contractor or volunteer
working with pupils. It is the governing document for pupil welfare decisions
and is the clause source cited in parent correspondence.

Where this Code conflicts with a statutory requirement, the statutory
requirement prevails and the discrepancy is logged with the Deputy Head
Pastoral within five working days.

## 2. Attendance and Punctuality

Every pupil must attend every session unless a statutory exemption applies.
The school day is taken to start at 07:45 and registers close at 08:00.

Where a pupil's rolling attendance over any half-term falls below 85 per cent,
the following actions are mandatory and must be evidenced in the pupil file:

1. An attendance review meeting convened with the parent or guardian.
2. A written support plan naming the staff member accountable for follow-up.
3. Escalation to the Deputy Head Pastoral where attendance remains below
   threshold for a further four weeks.

Punctuality is treated as attendance for the purposes of this clause. Three
late marks in any one week are recorded as one unauthorised session.

## 3. Safeguarding Referrals

Any concern about a pupil's welfare is reported the same day through the
designated safeguarding channel. The welfare of the pupil is the paramount
consideration and overrides every operational or reputational concern.

Staff must not investigate a concern themselves. They record what was seen or
heard, and pass it on.

## 4. Pupil Health Centre

The on-site Pupil Health Centre is staffed during the school day and is
responsible for the health records of every pupil on roll.

Health records that the Centre must hold and keep current:

- a completed medical information form from the parent or guardian;
- a current immunisation record for the diseases listed in the national
  schedule, with expiry dates recorded where a course requires boosting;
- details of any prescribed medication held on site and the administering
  protocol.

Where an immunisation record is missing or expired, the Pupil Health Centre
issues a written notice to the parent or guardian setting a deadline of
fourteen calendar days. A pupil whose record remains incomplete after that
period may be excluded from off-site activities until the record is complete.

## 5. Behaviour and Pastoral Support

Pastoral support is preventive. Class teachers hold primary responsibility for
early identification of a pupil who is struggling, and for recording that
concern in the pupil file.

## 6. Medicines and First Aid

Medicines are administered only against a signed parental instruction and a
prescriber's note. First aid is recorded on the day it is given.

## 7. Bursary and Fee Collection

Fees are invoiced termly in advance and are due within fourteen days of the
invoice date. The Bursary is responsible for the fee ledger and for all
parent communication about overdue balances.

Where a balance remains unpaid sixty days after the due date, the following
actions are mandatory:

1. A written reminder from the Bursary to the fee payer.
2. A payment plan offered where the family requests one in writing.
3. Referral to the Deputy Head Pastoral where a balance remains unresolved
   after ninety days, to assess the effect on the pupil.

No pupil is removed from roll, excluded from an activity or publicly
identified on account of an unpaid balance. Fee recovery is a commercial
matter between the school and the fee payer.

## 8. Complaints and Escalation

A parent or guardian who disagrees with a decision taken under this Code may
escalate in writing to the Head within ten working days.

## 9. Records and Retention

Records made under this Code are retained in accordance with the school's
retention schedule and are available to inspectors on request.

## 10. Review of this Code

This Code is reviewed annually by the Deputy Head Pastoral and ratified by the
governing body. Material changes are notified to parents before the start of
the half-term in which they take effect.
